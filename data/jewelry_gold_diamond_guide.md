# Gold Hallmarking, Diamond Certification & Purity Standards

## Gold Purity and BIS Hallmarking Standards
All our gold jewelry is 100% BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) 916 Hallmarked (22 Karat - 91.6% pure gold) or 18 Karat (75.0% pure gold). Each gold ornament is laser-engraved with a unique 6-digit HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification Number), verifiable on the official BIS Care app.

## Natural Diamond Certification
Every solitaire and diamond piece above 0.30 carats is independently certified by the Gemological Institute of America (GIA) or International Gemological Institute (IGI). We exclusively source conflict-free natural diamonds with minimum VVS-VS clarity and D-to-G colour grade ratings.

## Tamper-Evident Security Packaging
All jewelry items are sealed in tamper-evident security vault cases with laser serial seals before armored dispatch.
