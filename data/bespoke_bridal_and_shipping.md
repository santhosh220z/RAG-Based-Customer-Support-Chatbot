# Bespoke Bridal, Custom Tailoring, and Armored Logistics Policy

## Bespoke Bridal Wear & Custom Tailoring
- Custom bridal lehengas, bespoke sherwanis, and stitched silk saree blouses require a 3-to-5 week artisan production window.
- Because bespoke garments are custom-tailored to individual measurements, custom stitched items are non-returnable. However, we provide up to 2 complimentary alteration fittings at any of our flagship boutiques.

## High-Value Insured Armored Shipping
- All domestic jewelry orders over $1,000 are dispatched via armored high-security logistics (Brinks / Malca-Amit / FedEx Secure).
- 100% in-transit transit insurance is included at no additional fee.
- A government-issued photo ID and one-time delivery verification code (OTP) are required at delivery handover.
