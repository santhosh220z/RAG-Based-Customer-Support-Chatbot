# Textile, Pure Silk, Handloom & Cashmere Fabric Care Guide

## Pure Mulberry & Kanchipuram Silk Saree Care
1. **Dry Clean Only**: Never machine wash pure silk or handloom sarees. Always opt for professional hydrocarbon dry cleaning.
2. **Preservation & Storage**: Wrap pure silk sarees in breathable unbleached muslin or cotton cloth bags. Never store in plastic covers.
3. **Zari Maintenance**: Pure silver/gold electroplated zari borders must be folded inward to prevent oxidation and friction. Aerate silk sarees in mild shade every 6 months to preserve natural fabric luster.
4. **Ironing**: Use low-to-medium silk heat settings. Always iron on the reverse side with a damp protective pressing cloth.

## Kashmiri Pashmina & Pure Cashmere Shawl Care
1. **Washing**: Hand wash gently in lukewarm water (below 30°C) using mild liquid wool detergent or baby shampoo. Do not wring or twist.
2. **Drying**: Lay flat on a clean dry towel away from direct sunlight. Never tumble dry or hang wet pashmina.
