# Lifetime Jewelry Exchange, Buyback, and Resizing Policy

## Lifetime Gold & Diamond Exchange
- **Gold Exchange**: 100% valuation credit on the prevailing daily market rate of net gold metal weight (0% deduction on gold value).
- **Diamond Exchange**: 100% exchange credit against prevailing diamond price upon presentation of original GIA/IGI certificate.

## Lifetime Buyback Policy (Cash Refunds)
- 90% of prevailing market rate for diamonds and gold net weight.
- Making charges, custom design fees, and taxes are non-refundable in buyback.

## Complimentary Ring & Bangle Resizing
We offer 1 free resizing for rings and bangles within 60 days of purchase. Subsequent resizing or resizing involving added metal/diamonds is billed at direct material cost.
